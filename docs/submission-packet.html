Submission text (from submission-writeup-draft.md, signed off Oct 31, 2026)

What it is

Rankall scans a business's visibility across AI platforms and tells it whether AI recommends it or its competitors. For this hackathon we built the next step: from a real scan, Rankall generates a personalized video report — a presenter who tells that business, from its own real numbers, where AI recommends it and where competitors are taking its customers. One click claims the full written report.

What was built Oct 26 – Nov 4 (in-window work — the submission)

  • Video pipeline: scan aggregates → script → director verification → narration → avatar render → delivery page, with a durable job store and checkpoint/resume so a 60-second serverless limit never loses a render.
  • Meta AI as the 12th scanned platform, live by default, via Meta's Model API (Muse Spark 1.3). New scans run 12 platforms; pre-Meta demos keep their original platform count (the Oct 8 Folsom demo shows 7 of 11, with no Meta line — nothing backfilled).
  • Agentic director: an agent layer inside the pipeline that reviews its own draft against the real scan JSON, rejects weak hooks and overclaims, and repairs them before rendering.
  • Judge Mode: 11 pre-scanned real businesses across 7 segments with instantly playing videos, plus a visible Meta chain and on-page director report.
  • Delivery page + jobs dashboard + self-serve front door (Business / Person toggle, one shared scan pipeline).
  • Bounded cross-invocation script retries: when Spark returns empty content, the job stays processing and a later poll retries Spark (max 3 attempts total), instead of falling back immediately. Proven live: two empties, then a Spark script on the third attempt.
  • Product identification — prototype note (design note, not a tracking claim): Muse (Meta AI) identifies products, and Rankall checks whether it identifies YOUR product correctly, misidentifies it, or substitutes a competitor's product — ad vs organic never mixed. The Judge Mode page carries a short labeled note; the demo hero stays the low scorer. Full cross-platform tracking is post-Nov 4 work.

Pre-existing (NOT hackathon work)

The scan engine, platform scoring, reports, dashboards, and business pages existed before Oct 26 and appear only as context. The business page is a labeled secondary link ("this page already existed — the video is what's new"). No new business-page building happened in-window.

How it works (architecture, in words)

A real scan produces aggregates only — raw AI response text is dropped by an allowlist, even nested, and NULLs stay null, never backfilled. Muse Spark 1.3 writes the script; the director agent checks every number against the scan JSON and rejects overclaims; ElevenLabs (Eric voice) narrates; HeyGen v3 (Dante avatar) renders; the delivery page shows the video, the numbers, and one claim CTA. A job checkpoints after every stage in Supabase (input, script, director, narration asset, render-start, render-complete); a later poll resumes from the last checkpoint; a started render is only re-polled, never re-started; a stall past 10 minutes with no progress fails honestly with the failed stage. When Spark returns no usable script, the video uses a flagged rule-based fallback — flagged on the page, not hidden.
